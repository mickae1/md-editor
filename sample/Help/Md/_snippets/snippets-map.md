## Map

The **map** shows the sectors, the beacons and the trajectories of the simulated flights.

<img src="images/map-overview.svg" alt="Map view" width="480" height="270">

| Command | Shortcut | Description |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Zoom | `Ctrl` + wheel | Zooms the view in or out |
| Pan | Middle click | Drags the map |
| Center | `C` | Centers on the selection |
